# Privacy Policy

**WODrounds** is a simple workout timer app.

## The app

- **No accounts.** There are no sign-in or user accounts. Timer settings and state stay on your device; they are not sent to us.
- **No ads and no tracking.** The app has no advertising, no tracking and no ID of any kind.
- **Anonymous crash reports and usage counts (iOS only):** The iOS app sends crash reports, whether a session ended in a crash, Apple's performance diagnostics, and a count each time a workout starts and finishes, to **Sentry** (a third-party service). The count's only detail is the mode (EMOM, Intervals or For Time). Each report carries the app version, the iOS version, the device model and the language setting. Every install reports as the same anonymous user: no installation ID, device ID or advertising ID is sent, Sentry is set not to store IP addresses, and nothing is kept on your device for measuring. It never includes your timer settings, your workouts or anything from Apple Health, and none of it is linked to you or used for tracking. See [Sentry’s privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

## This website (wodrounds.iamjarl.com)

- **Visit statistics:** Pages may load **Umami** analytics (hosted on our infrastructure) to collect **aggregate** visit data (e.g. which pages are viewed). This is used to understand traffic, not to sell data or run targeted ads. Umami is designed for privacy-friendly, first-party analytics.
- **No workout data:** The website does not access or receive anything from the WODrounds app.

## Apple Health (iOS)

If you grant permission, the app can save completed workouts to the Health app (activity type: HIIT). That data stays in Health on your device and is not sent to us.
