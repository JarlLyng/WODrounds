Questions.

What is an EMOM timer?

EMOM stands for Every Minute on the Minute. You do a set at the start of each minute and rest for what is left of it. In WODrounds you choose 1 to 120 rounds, each from 30 seconds to 9 minutes 30 seconds long.

Can I use WODrounds for Tabata?

Yes. Tabata is 20 seconds of work and 10 seconds of rest, 8 times. Choose Intervals and set work to 20, rest to 10 and rounds to 8. The timer runs 3:50, because there is no rest after the last round.

Can I time a For Time WOD?

Yes. Choose For Time and the clock counts up from zero. Press Stop when you finish and the Done screen shows your time. You can set a time cap from 0:30 to 60:00, or leave it uncapped.

Does it work on Apple TV?

Yes. WODrounds runs on Apple TV, controlled with the Siri Remote, with the same three timers and the same sound cues as on the iPhone. It runs on its own there and does not sync with the iPhone.

Do I need an iPhone for the Apple Watch app?

Not for EMOM and Intervals, which the Watch sets up and runs on its own. A workout you start on the iPhone shows on the Watch live, with the same time and round, and that is how For Time reaches the Watch. The Watch gives haptics, not sound.

Does WODrounds keep a log of my workouts?

No. It is a timer, not a log. On iPhone and iPad it can save a finished workout to Apple Health as a HIIT session, if you allow it.

What does it cost?

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Apple TV. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. The App Store shows the price in your currency.

Does the app or this site track me?

The app has no analytics and no ad tracking. On iPhone and iPad it can send crash reports so I can fix bugs, which is the Diagnostics on the App Store's privacy label. This site counts page views with Umami, without cookies. Neither sees your workouts.

Which languages is the app in?

English, Danish and Spanish. The spoken cues are in English.
