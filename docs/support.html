Support

WODrounds is a minimal EMOM, interval and For Time timer available on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Apple TV.

Getting help

If you run into a bug or have a feature request, reach out through one of these channels:

  • In the app: open About (the ⓘ button) and tap Send feedback. It starts an email to us with the app and system version already filled in, and you see all of it before you send.
  • App Store: Use the Support URL on the WODrounds App Store page.
  • Email: support@iamjarl.com
  • GitHub: open an issue, if you would rather report it in the open.

What to include

We do not receive your timer settings or workout content from the app. On iOS, crash reports may reach us via Sentry. This website uses Umami for aggregate page statistics only. When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Which device and platform (e.g. iPhone 15, Apple TV 4K, Mac)
  • OS version (e.g. iOS 18, tvOS 18, macOS 15)
  • What you were doing when the issue occurred

Supported platforms

  • iPhone and iPad (iOS / iPadOS 17+)
  • Apple Watch (watchOS 10+)
  • Mac (macOS 13+ Ventura)
  • Apple TV (tvOS 17+)
