Support

WODrounds is a minimal EMOM and interval timer available on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Apple TV.

Getting help

If you run into a bug or have a feature request, reach out through one of these channels:

  • In-app: Open the About screen inside WODrounds and tap the support link.
  • App Store: Use the Support URL on the WODrounds App Store page.
  • Email: jarl@iamjarl.com

What to include

WODrounds does not collect any data or analytics, so we cannot access your usage. When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Which device and platform (e.g. iPhone 15, Apple TV 4K, Mac)
  • OS version (e.g. iOS 18, tvOS 18, macOS 15)
  • What you were doing when the issue occurred

Supported platforms

  • iPhone and iPad (iOS / iPadOS 17+)
  • Apple Watch (watchOS 10+)
  • Mac (macOS 14+)
  • Apple TV (tvOS 17+)
