Support

WODrounds is a minimal EMOM and Intervals timer for workouts.

Getting help

  • Bugs or feature requests: Use the support link in the app’s About screen (or the Support URL on the App Store).
  • General questions: Same — the About screen has the link to get in touch.

We do not collect data or use analytics, so we can’t access your usage. Please describe your device (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 17) and what you were doing when the issue occurred.
