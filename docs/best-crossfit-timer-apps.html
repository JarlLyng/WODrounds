Best CrossFit Timer Apps (2026)

By IAMJARL

There are a handful of solid timer apps for CrossFit and functional fitness. Each takes a different approach. This is an honest comparison of the most popular options, including WODrounds (which I built). I will be upfront about what each app does well and where it falls short.

SmartWOD Timer

Best for: athletes who need AMRAP and For Time modes alongside EMOM and Tabata.

SmartWOD is the most popular CrossFit timer with over half a million users. It supports every common WOD format: AMRAP, EMOM, For Time, Tabata and custom intervals. The interface uses large, colorful buttons and the app works on both iOS and Android. Apple Watch support is included.

  • Supports AMRAP and For Time (most timer apps do not)
  • iOS and Android
  • Free with ads, or paid to remove them
  • Large established community

Seconds Interval Timer

Best for: people who want maximum flexibility and complex interval sequences.

Seconds is a general-purpose interval timer that can be configured for almost any workout structure. You can chain multiple timers, set different sounds for each phase, and create detailed workout templates. It supports both iOS and Android.

  • Extremely flexible interval programming
  • Template library with community workouts
  • Subscription-based pricing
  • Can feel complex for simple EMOM or Tabata use

Box Timer

Best for: gyms and boxes that want a simple, free timer on a big screen.

Box Timer has a clean interface designed to look like the clock on a CrossFit box wall. It focuses on simplicity and is completely free. If you just need a straightforward countdown timer without extra features, this is a solid choice.

  • Free, no ads
  • Clean gym-clock aesthetic
  • Limited to basic timer modes
  • Web-based, works on any device with a browser

PushPress Timer

Best for: gym owners and coaches who program workouts for members.

PushPress is part of a larger gym management platform. The timer includes Death By, EMOM, Tabata, Work/Rest and Beep Test modes. It is aimed at coaches who need to program for a class rather than individual athletes.

  • Multiple specialized timer modes
  • Built for coaches and gym programming
  • Part of the PushPress ecosystem
  • More than most individual athletes need

WODrounds

Best for: athletes who want a minimal, private timer across all Apple devices.

This is my app. I built WODrounds because I wanted a timer that does two things well: EMOM (1–120 rounds, 0:30–9:30 round length) and intervals (work 5–60s, rest 5–60s, 1–120 rounds). No accounts, no ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases. No marketing trackers in the app; optional crash reporting on iOS via Sentry. It runs natively on 5 Apple platforms: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Apple TV. Start a workout on iPhone and the Watch syncs automatically via WatchConnectivity. Completed workouts are saved to Apple Health as HIIT sessions.

  • Native on every Apple platform (including Apple TV and Mac)
  • Real-time iPhone → Apple Watch sync
  • No account, no ads, no subscription — see Privacy for iOS crash reporting (Sentry) and site analytics (Umami)
  • Apple Health integration (workouts saved as HIIT)
  • Does not support AMRAP or For Time modes
  • Apple only (no Android or web version)

Which one should you pick?

If you need AMRAP and For Time modes, SmartWOD is the most complete option. If you want maximum customization and complex interval chains, Seconds gives you the most control. If you want a free, no-frills gym clock, Box Timer works in any browser.

If you train on Apple devices, want a timer that works on your Watch, Mac and Apple TV without accounts or ads, and you primarily do EMOM and interval workouts — that is exactly what I built WODrounds for.
